Examples:
Q: How many employees are in HR?

Q: Which department has the most employees?

Q: Who is the manager of Arun Nair?
SELECT e.employee_id, e.name AS employee, m.name AS manager FROM employees e LEFT JOIN employees m ON e.manager_id = m.employee_id WHERE e.name LIKE '%Arun Nair%'

Q: What is our profit this year?
SELECT revenue_inr, expenses_inr, vendor_costs_inr, revenue_inr - expenses_inr - vendor_costs_inr AS profit_inr FROM (SELECT (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(total_inr),0) FROM sales_invoices WHERE invoice_date BETWEEN '<<FY_START>>' AND '<<FY_END>>') AS revenue_inr, (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(amount_inr),0) FROM expenses WHERE expense_date BETWEEN '<<FY_START>>' AND '<<FY_END>>') AS expenses_inr, (SELECT COALESCE(SUM(amount_inr),0) FROM vendor_invoices WHERE invoice_date BETWEEN '<<FY_START>>' AND '<<FY_END>>') AS vendor_costs_inr)
